**Every rupee your family is owed — found, proven, claimed. Meet JAAGRUK (जागरूक).**

About ₹1 lakh crore sits unclaimed in Indian banks, insurers and dividend funds — and families miss schemes, scholarships
and deadlines because the information is scattered across hundreds of government portals. Scammers fill the gap.

We built **JAAGRUK**, an AI agent that works on the *live* government web:

🧮 **Haq Meter** — one number for everything a family is entitled to, with every scheme **proven** by a Z3 solver (and the exact rule + official source).
🔎 **Wapsi** — searches 7 unclaimed-money portals in parallel using @TinyFish: a compiled playbook on a TinyFish cloud browser for LIC, a live TinyFish web agent for insurers, and an assisted session where the citizen types their own OTP/CAPTCHA (we never solve CAPTCHAs).
🛡️ **Scam Shield** — paste a WhatsApp "yojana" message; ML + live PIB Fact Check evidence tells you if it's fake.
📡 **Change Radar** — reads official notice boards and alerts you only about deadlines that affect *your* family.
🗣️ Hindi + English, voice onboarding, no Aadhaar asked, nothing submitted without your approval.

Everything was tested against the real portals — and what broke made the product better: Indian portals need an
Indian IP, a "completed" agent run can hide a CAPTCHA, and eligibility rules must be re-verified against the live page.
